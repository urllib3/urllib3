Added ``urllib3.multipart.MultipartEncoder`` for streaming multipart uploads
and ``Part`` with buffered ``read()`` and ``peek()`` operations. Encoders can
rewind with ``seek(0, 0)`` when their body parts support seeking.
``encode_multipart_formdata()`` uses the encoder internally and continues to
return a complete bytes body.

Added ``MultipartDecoder`` for parsing complete multipart response bodies,
with raw part data and support for repeated headers.

Fixed a regression where unbracketed scoped IPv6 addresses passed to
``HTTPConnectionPool`` or ``HTTPSConnectionPool`` were rejected or incorrectly
normalized.
Fixed a regression where repeated normalization of IPv6 zone identifiers in
``ProxyManager`` caused the forwarded request target to disagree with the
``Host`` header.
Fixed a bug where IPv6 zone identifiers were incorrectly lowercased in
connection-pool keys and CONNECT targets.
